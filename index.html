S級任務

PERSONAL PROJECT · VOICE AI CHIEF OF STAFF

EDITH

A hands-free AI chief of staff that lives on my laptop. It starts with Windows and listens offline for "Hey EDITH", transcribes me locally with Whisper, then thinks with Claude, pulling from my Gmail, Calendar and Drive to summarize my inbox and keep my schedule on track.

It remembers facts between sessions, looks at my screen when asked, and answers out loud in a neural voice. Access is read-only by design, so a misheard command can't send or delete anything, and a local Ollama model takes over if Claude is unreachable.
